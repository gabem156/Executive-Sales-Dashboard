# Executive Sales Dashboard

## Dashboard Overview

This interactive Tableau dashboard provides an executive-level overview of sales performance, profitability, and product performance using the Sample Superstore dataset.

The dashboard was designed to help business leaders quickly monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify high-performing regions and product categories, and explore sales trends through interactive filtering.

---

## Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Quantity Sold

These KPIs provide a high-level summary of overall business performance.

---

### Sales by Region

Analyzes sales performance across geographic regions and highlights the highest-performing markets.

Business Question:

* Which regions generate the highest sales revenue?

---

### Profit by Category

Compares profitability across product categories.

Business Question:

* Which product categories contribute the most profit?

---

### Sales by Segment

Visualizes sales performance across customer segments.

Business Question:

* Which customer segments generate the most sales?

---

## Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters that allow users to dynamically explore the data.

Available Filters:

* Region
* Category

All dashboard visualizations and KPI metrics update automatically based on filter selections.

---

## Key Insights

* The West region generated the highest sales performance.
* Technology was the most profitable product category.
* Consumer customers contributed the largest share of sales.
* Dashboard filters allow users to drill into specific regions and product categories for deeper analysis.
